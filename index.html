Controls

Hands on the deck

  • WASD Move
  • Space Lightsaber
  • F or Shift Hero special
  • Q Use Force power
  • 1234 Select Force power
  • E Interact
  • Esc Pause

On a phone, turn sideways. The stick moves you. A flick of the stick still takes a step. The slash button swings. A saber-colored mark shows which way the swing goes. The gold special face is Hope Strike, Staff Spin, or Bowcaster Blast, and it dims while that toy cools down. A short tap on the power glyph uses the lit Force power. Hold it to switch to the next unlocked power, or tap a slot 1–4. Dark slots are locked, and Power stays dim until you own one. On a phone, Saber Throw bends toward the nearest foe. On the Docking Ring, Interact stays lit and reads Learn Force Push until the blue terminal teaches it. The north lock stays shut until then. After that, Interact stays dim until a salvage chest, side hatch, north lock, or chase pad is in reach, then the symbol names that thing. A quick tap on the move keys takes a step, and letting go of a walk stops you. If a chase fails or the deck downs you, press Space to retry. Pause opens the menu. Mute lives on the title and on this pause menu.
